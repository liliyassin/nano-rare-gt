## Active / Ongoing Gene Therapy Trials

### 1. NCT02599922 — AAV Gene Therapy for CNGB3 Achromatopsia (AGTC-401)
**Sponsor:** Beacon Therapeutics (formerly AGTC)
**Status:** Active, Not Recruiting
**Phase:** Phase 1/2
**Started:** April 2016
**Intervention:** Subretinal injection of AGTC-401 (AAV vector carrying CNGB3 gene), single eye

### 6. NCT01648452 — CNTF Implants for CNGB3 Achromatopsia
**Sponsor:** National Eye Institute (NEI)
**Status:** Completed
**Phase:** Phase 1/2
**Started:** July 2012
**Intervention:** Intraocular CNTF (ciliary neurotrophic factor) sustained-release implant — placed surgically into one eye.
**Approach:** CNTF is a neuroprotective/neurotrophic factor tested for retinal rescue rather than gene correction.
**Target population:** Adults ≥18 with confirmed CNGB3 mutations, BCVA ≤20/100.
**Note:** This is a pharmacological/protein-based approach, not gene replacement. Tests whether CNTF can improve/stabilise cone function.
**ClinicalTrials link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01648452

## Non-Gene Therapy / Pharmacological Trials

### 7. NCT04041232 — PBA (Glycerol Phenylbutyrate) for ATF6-/- Achromatopsia
**Sponsor:** Columbia University
**Status:** Suspended (as of 2025-04-08 start date)
**Phase:** Early Phase 1
**Started:** April 2025
**Intervention:** Oral glycerol phenylbutyrate (PBA) — an FDA-approved drug for urea cycle disorders — repurposed to reduce ER stress.
**Mechanism:** ATF6 mutations cause ER stress in photoreceptors → PBA acts as a chemical chaperone to reduce ER stress and potentially restore retinal function.
**Gene target:** ATF6 (a less common cause of achromatopsia, ~2% of cases)
**Dosing:** 3× daily, 4.5–11.2 mL/m²/day, ≥3 clinic visits with retinal function tests and imaging.
**Note:** Drug repurposing approach — distinct from the AAV gene therapy trials. Currently suspended.
**ClinicalTrials link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04041232

## Key Themes for Dissertation

**Gene targets:** CNGB3 (~50% of cases) and CNGA3 (~25%) dominate the trial landscape. ATF6 (~2%) has one early pharmacological trial.

**Delivery method:** All gene therapy trials use AAV (adeno-associated virus) vectors delivered by **subretinal injection** — this is the standard surgical approach for retinal gene therapy.

**Trial phases:** All interventional trials are Phase 1/2, reflecting the still-early clinical stage of achromatopsia GT.

**Main sponsors:** Two industry leaders — **Beacon Therapeutics** (CNGA3 + CNGB3) and **MeiraGTx** (CNGA3 + CNGB3) — with academic/NIH work on complementary approaches (CNTF, PBA, registries).

**Alternative approach:** The Columbia PBA trial is notable as a non-GT approach targeting ER stress in a rarer subtype (ATF6), currently suspended.

**Observational infrastructure:** Multiple completed and active natural history/registry studies (MeiraGTx, Beacon, NEI eyeGENE, FFB) providing phenotypic data that informed and continues to inform trial design.
